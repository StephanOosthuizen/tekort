Tekort Support

Something not working, or a question about your account

Write to reijger@support.co.za and you will get a reply.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by Tekort. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Tekort. Cancel before the period is out and you keep it to the end of what you have already paid for.

Restoring a subscription on a new phone

Open Tekort and tap Restore at the top of the pricing screen. It asks Apple what you have already bought.

Deleting your account

In Tekort, go to the You tab and choose Delete account. It cannot be undone, so take an Export everything first if you want a copy.

A product with wrong numbers

Tekort's food database is built by the people using it, so a product can be wrong. Open it and use Report. Enough reports and it goes back into the queue to be checked.
